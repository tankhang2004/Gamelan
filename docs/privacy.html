The short version

Yuk, Nari! does not collect your personal data. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. Everything the camera sees is processed on your device and thrown away. Nothing is uploaded to us, because there is no server of ours to upload it to.

Camera

The game needs the front camera to read how you move. Each frame is passed to Apple's on-device Vision framework, which returns the positions of your joints, and the game compares those positions against the move it is asking for. The frame itself is then discarded.

Camera frames are never written to storage, never sent off your device, and never seen by us. Nothing from the camera is used to identify you. The game only runs the camera while a play session is on screen; leaving the session stops it.

You are asked for camera permission the first time you play, and you can withdraw it at any time in the Settings app. Without it, the game cannot run.

Session recordings

While a run is in progress, the game can record the screen — the camera picture and the game overlay together — so you can watch the run back on the results screen. Recording uses Apple's ReplayKit, which asks for your permission before it begins. The microphone is not recorded.

The clip is written to storage that belongs to this app on your device. We never receive it. It is deleted when a new run replaces it or when you delete the app. You choose what happens to it:

  • Save — the clip is copied to your photo library. The app asks for add-only photo access for this, which lets it add the clip but not read anything already in your library.
  • Share — iOS's own share sheet opens and you pick the destination. Anything you send from there is governed by the privacy policy of whichever app or service you choose.
  • Neither — the clip stays on the device until it is replaced.

Scores and settings

Your past runs, your best score, your music and sound-effect levels, and your chosen language are stored locally on your device. They are not transmitted anywhere and are removed when you delete the app.

Game Center

The app offers an optional worldwide leaderboard through Apple's Game Center. If — and only if — you are signed in to Game Center, your final score for a run is submitted to that leaderboard, where your Game Center nickname and score are visible to other players.

Game Center is operated by Apple, not by us. We receive no personal information through it beyond the leaderboard entries you can see for yourself in the app. Apple's handling of this data is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy. If you do not sign in to Game Center, no score leaves your device.

No tracking, no advertising

The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no third-party code that reports on your use of it. It does not use the Advertising Identifier, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell or share data with anyone.

Children

The game is made with young dancers in mind. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included, and asks for no name, email address, or account. The one feature that puts anything on the internet — the Game Center leaderboard — is optional and is governed by the Game Center settings on the device, which a parent can control through Screen Time.

External links

The information screen links to Mekar Bhuana Centre, the Balinese music and dance community that inspired the choreography in this game. Opening that link leaves the app for your web browser, and their website has its own privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
