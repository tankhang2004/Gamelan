Yuk, Nari!

An exercise game inspired by Balinese dance — your body is the controller.

Support Privacy Policy

What it is

Yuk, Nari! turns the most basic movements of Balinese dance into a game you play with your whole body. Stand your iPhone or iPad upright on the floor, step back until you are fully in frame, and dance. The front camera reads your movement — there is nothing to tap while you play.

You walk in a steady left-right head tilt to build Taksu, your energy meter. Miss what the game asks of you and Taksu drains. When it empties, the run is over. There is no finish line — only a higher score.

How to play

  • Ngayog — walkWalk in place, tilting your head gently left and right, like a dancer. Every full cycle earns points and energy.
  • FrangipaniBlossoms drift into the room as you walk. Reach out or step over to collect them for extra points.
  • Nge'ed — squatWhen the game calls for it, squat and hold while the wave passes. Don't stop walking.
  • Leyak — dodgeA Leyak dives down the room at wherever you're standing. Get out of its column in time.
  • Agem — freezeMatch the held pose, right or left, and hold it. The fuller your Taksu, the more each second is worth.

What you need

  • iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later, with a front camera
  • Room to stand — roughly two metres of clear floor, so the camera sees you head to toe
  • A place to stand the device upright, facing you
  • Available in Bahasa Indonesia and English

Support

Get in touch

Questions, bugs, or feedback on the tracking — email tankhang191104@gmail.com. This is a small independent project, so please allow a few days for a reply.

When reporting a tracking problem, it helps to include your device model, iOS version, and what the game was asking you to do at the time.

The game says it can't see me.
Your whole body has to be in frame, head to toe, before a run will start. Move the device further back, or set it lower — on the floor works best. A room with even light and a plain wall behind you tracks far better than a backlit window.
I turned the camera down and now the game won't start.
Open the Settings app, find Yuk, Nari! in the list, and switch Camera back on. The game reads your movement from the camera and cannot run without it.
My squats and poses aren't being counted.
Check that no part of you leaves the frame mid-move, and that you're facing the device straight on. Loose or very dark clothing against a dark background makes you harder to read. Pose detection is still being tuned, so if a move you're doing correctly won't register, please email us — that feedback is genuinely useful.
The leaderboard is empty.
The world leaderboard runs on Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app, then reopen the leaderboard. Your own past runs are kept on the device and appear whether or not you're signed in.
Can I keep a video of my run?
Yes. At the end of a run you can play the clip back and save it to your photo library or share it. The clip is recorded on your device and is never uploaded anywhere by the game.
How do I change the language or turn the music down?
Tap the gear icon on the main menu. Music and sound effects have separate volume sliders, and you can switch between Bahasa Indonesia and English at any time.
